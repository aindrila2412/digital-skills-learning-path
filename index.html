    About

    This is a personal portfolio project by Aindrila Das: a free, open 8-week course outline on digital, cloud and AI skills, with this companion app. It is not an accredited programme, and it does not issue certificates. Any sample data in the repository is fictional.

    Privacy

    The app works offline, has no accounts, and sends nothing anywhere. Your ticks, quiz scores and check-ins are saved in your browser's storage only.

    Accessibility and low data

    • The whole app is around 100 KB and uses no web fonts or images except small icons.
    • Use the text size buttons below. Colour is never the only signal.
    • Light colours by default. You can switch to dark, or follow your device, below. It also respects the reduced-motion setting.
